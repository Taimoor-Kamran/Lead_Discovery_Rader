Residential services

From a flickering kitchen light to a full rewire, our licensed electricians diagnose the fault, explain it in plain words and give you a written price before any work starts. We install ceiling fans, outdoor lighting, dedicated circuits for appliances and car chargers, and we replace old panels that can no longer carry a modern household's load.

Commercial services

For offices, shops and restaurants we plan lighting and power around how the space is used, schedule work outside trading hours where we can, and keep the paperwork for inspections in order.

New construction and major renovations

We work alongside builders and homeowners from the first drawings to the final inspection, so the wiring behind the walls is right the first time.
