Branch: `spec/v0.13.0-domain-intel`
- **Base:** `main` @ `beacf47` (v0.12.0 merged, `AUDIT_LOGIC_VERSION = 8`, schema 0013)
- **Author:** Taimoor
- **Status:** draft

---

## Goal

Add a per-domain evidence axis built from DNS and RDAP that does not depend on fetching the
homepage, so a business whose site answers with a bot challenge, a non-2xx page, or nothing
at all still produces true, citable findings.

Today 21 of the 147 production businesses sit at `bot_challenge`, `not_readable` or
`unreachable` and emit zero or near-zero findings. DNS and RDAP answer for all of them.

Two rules govern everything below:

1. **Certainty or silence.** A DNS answer of `NOERROR` with no record of that type means the
   record is genuinely absent → emit. `SERVFAIL`, `REFUSED`, timeout, or any transport error
   means unknown → store `null`, emit nothing. A failure that reads like an answer is the same
   defect shape as the Cloudflare pages in v0.12.0.
2. **No people.** Business-level registry facts only. Registrant name, email, phone and
   postal address are dropped at the parse boundary and never stored, logged or fixtured.

---

## Scope

- `core/dns.py` — resolver behind an injectable interface, plus a fixture-backed resolver.
- `core/rdap.py` — RDAP over HTTPS, rate-limited and daily-capped like Places and PSI.
- `domain_intel` cache table keyed by registrable domain, plus an Alembic migration.
- The full snapshot copied into the audit's `checks` so findings stay reproducible from the
  audit row alone.
- Nine new finding codes with `CATALOGUE` entries.
- Per-status allow-list changes so domain findings survive `bot_challenge`, `not_readable`
  and `unreachable`.
- New settings, all with defaults.
- Tests with fixtures recorded from real domains among the 147, plus one live smoke script.

## Out of scope

- DKIM — selectors are not discoverable, so absence can never be proven.
- Parked-domain inference.
- SPF recursive lookup counting (the 10-lookup limit).
- DNSSEC validation, RBL/blacklist checks, IP geolocation, reverse DNS, zone transfer.
- Port-43 WHOIS. DNS-over-HTTPS.
- Raising `AUDIT_LOGIC_VERSION` to 9 — see **Blockers**.
- Any frontend work. The new findings render through the existing `FindingList`.
- Any registrant or personal contact data, in any form, for any reason.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A DNS query answering `NOERROR` with no record of that type produces the finding. The same
   query answering `SERVFAIL`, `REFUSED`, timing out, or `NXDOMAIN` on the apex produces
   `null` and **no** finding. Proven by one test per response code.
2. The resolver actually used is recorded in the stored snapshot.
3. An RDAP fixture containing registrant name, email, phone and address results in a stored
   `domain_intel` row and a stored audit `checks` blob containing none of those four values.
   Asserted against the stored row, not against the parser's return value.
4. A business whose latest audit status is `bot_challenge` still receives its domain findings,
   and those findings can support an opportunity.
5. A business on a builder subdomain, and a business whose only URL is a social profile,
   produce zero DNS queries and zero RDAP calls.
6. `domain_no_a_record` never appears on an audit that fetched a 2xx page.
7. Every one of the nine finding codes has a fixture recorded from a real domain among the 147,
   and the fixture fails against the code as it stands before this spec.
8. Running the audit twice within `DNS_INTEL_TTL_DAYS` issues DNS queries only on the first run.
9. With `DNS_ENABLED=false` and `RDAP_ENABLED=false`, the full existing test suite passes
   unchanged.
10. `AUDIT_LOGIC_VERSION` is still 8 at merge.

## Implementation notes

### Finding codes

| Code | Severity | Service | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `domain_expired` | high | `website_design` | RDAP expiry date is in the past |
| `domain_no_a_record` | high | `website_design` | site host has no A, no AAAA and no CNAME, all `absent`, and no 2xx page in this audit |
| `domain_expiring_soon` | medium | `website_design` | expiry within `AUDIT_DOMAIN_EXPIRY_WARN_DAYS` |
| `multiple_spf_records` | medium | `null` | more than one apex TXT beginning `v=spf1` |
| `spf_allows_all` | medium | `null` | SPF `all` mechanism qualified `+` or unqualified |
| `no_spf` | low | `null` | apex TXT `absent` of any `v=spf1` record |
| `no_dmarc` | low | `null` | `_dmarc` TXT `absent` of any `v=DMARC1` record |
| `dmarc_policy_none` | low | `null` | DMARC record present with `p=none` |
| `no_domain_mx` | low | `null` | apex MX `absent` |

Only the three `website_design` codes map to something FlexTBS sells. The six email codes
carry `service: null` on purpose: they are context for the rep, they create no opportunity,
and they never reach the CRM as a claim FlexTBS cannot service. If the client later says
otherwise, that is a new spec.

Nothing above `medium` unless the answer is certain, and nothing above `high` at all.

### Wording

Messages state the registry or DNS fact and stop. No inference about the business.
`domain_expired` reads as *"Registry records show the domain registration expired on <date>."*
— not *"the domain is expired"*, which would be a claim about a state we cannot see.

### Provider mapping

NS and MX hosts are mapped to a provider name (builder, registrar, Google Workspace,
Microsoft 365) and stored as facts in the snapshot. No finding, no inference, no opportunity.

### Sequencing

Domain intelligence runs after the fetch outcome is known, because `domain_no_a_record` needs
the contradiction guard and the site host comes from `final_url`. It runs regardless of whether
the fetch succeeded.

## Blockers

- **`AUDIT_LOGIC_VERSION` stays at 8.** Bumping it to 9 makes all 147 businesses due
  immediately. Opportunities are never withdrawn when a later audit stops supporting them
  (v0.12.1 item), so a forced re-audit would manufacture a new batch of orphans on top of the
  95 that already exist. It would also cost 147 PageSpeed calls against a 200/day cap that was
  already exceeded (218 calls in 24 hours) during the v0.12.0 verification. The bump is a
  one-line follow-up once v0.12.1 lands.
- **95 orphaned pending opportunities are live in the review queue now** and are approvable by
  a reviewer. Not caused by this spec, but this spec's canary re-audit will move businesses
  between statuses again.
- **RDAP endpoint coverage is per-TLD.** `.com` and `.net` are well served. A TLD with no
  endpoint stores `null` and emits nothing; that is correct behaviour, not a failure.
