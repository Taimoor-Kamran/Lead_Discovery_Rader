## Goal

Two defects, both live in production, both about findings being wrong or stale rather than missing.

1. **An opportunity is never withdrawn when the audit that supported it stops supporting it.** 78 orphaned opportunities (38 `rules`, 40 `rules_and_ai`; corrected from 95 by Taimoor's production query) are pending and approvable in the review queue right now, citing findings that no longer exist. This contradicts the founding blueprint's core promise: a reviewer trusts a lead because they can see what it rests on.
2. **Booking is inferred from a link's text.** 56 businesses matched on a CTA phrase or a path with no booking tool found. 6 matched on `request a quote` or `request service`, which is not scheduling under any reading. 10 more matched on `schedule …`, and the one case verified by hand — ATX Electrical Services — goes to a Gravity Forms contact page with no date or time input anywhere on it. The client verified that gap himself and wrote it into his ATX report.

Also in scope: the DNS blip that froze the job queue for 30 minutes on 2026-09-28, because it is a settings change and a frozen queue means nothing above runs at all.

Production counts to work from: 465 `pending` opportunities, 2 `approved`.

## Scope

- `withdrawn_at` and `withdrawn_reason` on `opportunities`, plus a migration.
- Withdrawal in the upsert path; queue and CRM exclude withdrawn rows.
- A one-off backfill for the existing 78.
- Booking: drop the `request *` phrases; a CTA or path match is verified by fetching its target once (correction D).
- Engine, Redis and `pg_dump` timeouts.
- A targeted re-audit of the businesses that lost a booking opportunity.

## Out of scope

- A `withdrawn` value in `review_status`. The blueprint defines six human decisions (approve, reject, request enrichment, mark duplicate, not a fit, do not contact) and the code implements all six. Withdrawal is a system action, not a human decision, so it gets its own columns.
- Following links beyond the one booking CTA/path target (correction D allows exactly one page). A general crawl is M1 and is not in this spec.
- Address comparison on a real library. Still queued; it is silent, not wrong.
- A re-audit UI. Still queued.
- Any change to `AUDIT_LOGIC_VERSION` beyond what task 4 requires.
- Any new finding code.

## Implementation notes

**Why withdrawal is not a review status.** The blueprint (slide 41) fixes the six human decisions, and the code implements all six. Adding a seventh would put a system action in a list of human ones, and it collides with the pending-only unique index. Separate columns keep the audit trail the blueprint requires (slide 29: every stage transition timestamped and logged) without touching the decision model.

**Why the booking change is not a regression fix.** The founding blueprint's deterministic check list says "booking / scheduling link present" — a link check, which is what the code does. The Deep Audit Layer brief's M4 raised the bar to a working path, and the client's own ATX verification treats a contact-form-only "Schedule Now" as a real gap. The requirement moved; the check hasn't. Say it that way to the client.

**Production breakdown to work from.** 56 businesses matched booking by phrase or path: 51 by CTA text, 5 by path. Of those, 6 matched `request *`. Two matched real external booking hosts (Fresha, and `pd.aura.site`) — note that neither vendor is in the existing 15-widget signature list.

**Implementation (2026-10-01).**
- Withdrawal: `opportunities/service.py` `withdraw_unsupported` / `withdrawal_reasons`, run
  after the upsert and on `classify`'s early return. `withdrawn_reason` is
  `service_absent: …` and/or `findings_absent: …`, each naming the audit id.

  time, datetime-local, month or week input; no select; reCAPTCHA plus an iframe with no

  target reads fine, shows no scheduler signal, and has no onward booking links.
- Vendor hosts: the widget fingerprint hosts plus `fresha.com`, `phorest.com` and

**Correction G from Taimoor (2026-10-01). Found from a real page after the rule was
written, not by the tests.** ATX's real contact page has a footer credit, "Website Crafted
by Enlightened Owl Digital" → enlightenedowl.com. Under correction F's "a link to another
host" rule, ATX became `unverified_multi_hop` and its finding was silenced: the exact case
this fix exists to catch. The constructed fixture passed only because it did not have the
link. Replaced: a bare outside link is never evidence of onward booking. A link counts as
onward only when **(a)** its text or href contains a booking word as a whole word (book,
booking, appointment, schedule, reserve, and their plurals and forms; `facebook` and
`bookkeeping` do not match), or **(b)** the page offers a choice of locations: two or more
links whose text or href reads like a branch, store, salon, location or city, or a select
of two or more options named for one. Designer credits, trade bodies, badges, directories,
social and Google are ignored wherever they sit on the page. The footer credit is now in
the ATX fixture, with a test that it does not silence the finding. That test fails under
correction F's rule.
